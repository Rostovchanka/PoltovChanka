# The Caretaker — researched release cover catalogue

The software includes **22 distinct releases, stages, mixes or archival compilations** (1999–2019). This catalogue covers the principal fronts and titles, but *not every reissue-specific alternative sleeve*. Where artwork has not been verified, it is marked as such. No cover image files are included.

Sources used: [The Caretaker official Bandcamp](https://thecaretaker.bandcamp.com/music), [The Caretaker discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Caretaker_(musician)), [The Caretaker art chronology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Everywhere_at_the_End_of_Time), [Boomkat records](https://boomkat.com), and individual release/cover references. The six main *Everywhere at the End of Time* painting names are attributed to Ivan Seal.


## 1. Selected Memories From The Haunted Ballroom (1999)

**Cover / artwork:** Photographic cover; edition artwork varies

**Observed visual language:** Archival, blue-monochrome ballroom photograph with dated print contrast and prominent typography.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Orchestra cyanotype, Ballroom paper whites, Period-photo compression, Midnight-blue shadows, Archival separation.

## 2. A stairway to the stars (2001)

**Cover / artwork:** Artwork attribution: Andrew Cliff (early release)

**Observed visual language:** Early haunted-ballroom era sleeve. Edition artwork differs; designed here as a restrained low-light archival treatment.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Ballroom cobalt, Ghost-room whites, Vintage paper fade, Dark-stage floor, Stairway isolation.

## 3. We'll all go riding on a rainbow (2003)

**Cover / artwork:** Graphic ballroom illustration

**Observed visual language:** Graphic ballroom silhouettes in electric blue/cyan, white and black; much higher contrast than the later oil paintings.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Electric-cyan silhouettes, Porcelain cutouts, Hard-black staging, Graphic midtone removal, Dance-floor separation.

## 4. Theoretically pure anterograde amnesia (2005–2006)

**Cover / artwork:** Dark abstract photographic design

**Observed visual language:** Dark purple/blue abstract cover with a bright vertical highlight and fragmented text.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Violet-blue fracture, Vertical-light bleach, Low-mid collapse, Indigo shadow mass, Abstraction separation.

## 5. Additional amnesiac memories (2006)

**Cover / artwork:** Edited Take Your Partners photograph (attributed in discography)

**Observed visual language:** Black-and-white reworking of a period ballroom-record image, visually linked to earlier Caretaker sleeves.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Amnesiac purple, Archival luminosity, Soft black suspension, Uncertain midtones, Companion-image isolation.

## 6. Deleted scenes, forgotten dreams (2007)

**Cover / artwork:** the impossible dream (Guy Denning, 2006)

**Observed visual language:** Painted cover by Guy Denning; figurative and emotionally charged, from the period before the Ivan Seal collaboration.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Deleted-scene greys, Forgotten highlight, Muted print color, Shadow reservation, Empty-frame separation.

## 7. Recollected memories from the Museum of Garden History (2008)

**Cover / artwork:** Artwork not independently verified

**Observed visual language:** Live archival release; precise front-cover imagery is edition-dependent and not fully verified.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Museum olive, Archival white, Botanical matte, Display-case shadow, Historical isolation.

## 8. Persistent repetition of phrases (2008)

**Cover / artwork:** Empty frame / blue-wall artwork (painting title not verified)

**Observed visual language:** Dark blue-violet, nearly empty interior: a faint rectangular picture frame hanging on a softly lit wall.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Blue frame illumination, Unoccupied wall whites, Violet interior cast, Empty-frame shadows, Frame isolation.

## 9. Recollections from old London town (2009)

**Cover / artwork:** Artwork not independently verified

**Observed visual language:** Radio/compilation archive. No single fully verified front cover for all circulating editions.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Old-London brown, Fogged print white, Street-photo compression, Archive shadow, Distance isolation.

## 10. FACT Mix 45: The Caretaker — Delving Into My Past for FACT (2009)

**Cover / artwork:** FACT mix sleeve / magazine graphic

**Observed visual language:** Promotional FACT mix design in purple-black with title lettering; mixes can be circulated in alternate formats.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** FACT-era grey, Distant electric blue, Print loss, Deep archive blacks, Mix-cover isolation.

## 11. The complete digital collection (1996–2008) (2009)

**Cover / artwork:** Compilation cover varies

**Observed visual language:** Collection edition: artwork and compilation packaging vary; no single sleeve is asserted here.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Collected-neutral palette, Early/late contrast, Archive fade, Subdued history blacks, Collection isolation.

## 12. An empty bliss beyond this World (2011)

**Cover / artwork:** happy in spite (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal: large pale blue-grey spherical object and a tiny red-tipped match against a dark studio background.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Pale plaster sphere, Match-point red, Blue-grey object, Dark studio backdrop, Object isolation.

## 13. Patience (After Sebald) (2012)

**Cover / artwork:** Ivan Seal glass-dome / lighthouse cover

**Observed visual language:** Dark painted snow-globe/lighthouse study; reflective dome, muted ground and miniature interior.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Snow-globe sheen, Lighthouse-white remnants, Glass darkness, Cold island ground, Dome isolation.

## 14. Extra Patience (After Sebald) (2012)

**Cover / artwork:** Ivan Seal glass-dome / lighthouse cover

**Observed visual language:** Companion dark-glass lighthouse cover with streaked/reflected marks around the dome.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Dark glass reflections, Wet-dome streaks, Lighthouse remnant, Black-glass depth, Object isolation.

## 15. Everywhere at the end of time — Stage 1 (2016)

**Cover / artwork:** Beaten Frowns After (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal still life: folded, rolled grey paper or scroll-like object against a blue-to-green backdrop.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Grey folded-paper tone, Sky-blue background, Muted green ground, Soft paper relief, Scroll-like isolation.

## 16. Everywhere at the end of time — Stage 2 (2017)

**Cover / artwork:** Pittor Pickgown in Khatheinstersper (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal still life: wilting flowers in an ambiguous decaying vase on a pale green/beige background.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Wilted-petal pink, Corroded vase shadow, Pastel green surround, Faded plant relief, Bouquet isolation.

## 17. Everywhere at the end of time — Stage 3 (2017)

**Cover / artwork:** Hag (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal: dense tangled kelp/plant-like shape, deep near-black green against a pale cool field.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Tangled deep green, Kelp-black lattice, Cool pale surround, Botanical detail loss, Growth isolation.

## 18. Take care. It’s a desert out there… (2017)

**Cover / artwork:** Pale abstract sculpture (Ivan Seal; painting title not verified)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal: pale grey abstract sculptural formation floating above a neutral light ground; the cover does not depict a literal desert.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Pale sculpture greys, Soft white surround, Mineral midtones, Floating cast-shadow, Object isolation.

## 19. Everywhere at the end of time — Stage 4 (2018)

**Cover / artwork:** Giltsholder (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal: blue-green disfigured human bust/profile with indistinct painted features.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Blue-green bust, Facial-form erosion, Oil-paint cobalt, Indistinct feature dark, Figure isolation.

## 20. Everywhere at the end of time — Stage 5 (2018)

**Cover / artwork:** Eptitranxisticemestionscers Desending (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal: multicolored thickly painted abstract accumulation, heavily fragmented with muted pinks, blues and greens.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Impasto muted rose, Fragmented cold blues, Painted-edge erosion, Compressed pigment range, Mass isolation.

## 21. Everywhere at the end of time — Stage 6 (2019)

**Cover / artwork:** Necrotomigaud (Ivan Seal)

**Observed visual language:** Ivan Seal: pale ochre-brown upright board with a spare blue-tape frame against a muted green background.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Ochre board flatness, Blue-tape remnant, Moss-grey backdrop, Object paint loss, Board isolation.

## 22. Everywhere, an empty bliss (2019)

**Cover / artwork:** Dark blue impasto abstract (Ivan Seal; edition variants)

**Observed visual language:** Dense dark oil-painted abstract field, with entangled strokes of deep blue, near-black, olive and muted high-chroma flecks.

**Five dedicated editable controls:** Deep blue impasto, Oil-black paint mass, Olive pigment flecks, Color fragment exhaustion, Abstract-field isolation.
